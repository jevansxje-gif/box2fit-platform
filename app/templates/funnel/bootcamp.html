Before you sign up

Questions, answered.

I haven't trained in years. Is this for me?

Yes. Your coach scales every round up or down, so you start where you are.

Will I get hit?

No. There's no sparring and no head contact. You work on pads and bags with a coach.

What do I need to bring?

Water and runners. Gloves and wraps are provided.

Why do you need my details now?

So the gym can call you and book your first class, and so your waiver is done before you arrive. No card details are taken until after your first class, and only if you want to keep going.
