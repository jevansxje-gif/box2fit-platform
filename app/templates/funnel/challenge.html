Before you sign up

Questions, answered.

I haven't trained in years. Is this for me?

Yes. That's who it's built for. Your coach sets your starting point in the personal session, and every round can be scaled up or down.

Will I get hit?

No. There's no sparring and no head contact. You work on pads and bags with a coach.

What if I can't make every morning?

Come to as many of the {{ c.class_time }} sessions as you can; three a week is plenty. If {{ c.class_time }} doesn't work at all, tell us which time would, just below.

What happens after the five weeks?

Your membership carries on at {{ renew_price }} + GST every 4 weeks from {{ c.renew.strftime('%B %d') }} unless you cancel before then. We remind you three days ahead with a one-click cancel link.

How is "most improved" decided?

By your coach, against the goals and starting measures you set together in your personal session.
